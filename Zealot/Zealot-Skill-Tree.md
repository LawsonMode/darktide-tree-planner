# Darktide — Zealot Skill Tree Reference

Transcribed from the 77 in-game tooltip screenshots in `Tree Descriptions/`. Each entry is the node **name**, **type**, whether it was shown **Locked** at capture time, and the **verbatim description**. A handful of tooltips were partially covered by a Windows Snipping Tool popup in the capture; those are flagged `[obscured]` and reconstructed where the standard wording is known.

> Node **types** in the Zealot tree: **Keystone** (one of 3, mutually exclusive), **Keystone Modifier** (augments the chosen keystone), **Ability / Combat Ability** (your F ability, mutually exclusive), **Ability Modifier**, **Blitz** (grenade, mutually exclusive), **Aura** (coherency buff, mutually exclusive), and **Passive** (stat/effect nodes).

## 1. Keystones (pick ONE — selecting one locks the others)

| Node | Description |
|---|---|
| **Inexorable Judgement** | Moving grants you Momentum (stacks 20 times). When you Hit an Enemy, spend all Momentum, gaining +1% Melee Attack Speed, +1% Ranged Attack Speed, and +1% Damage per stack. Selecting this Keystone locks all other Keystones. |
| **Blazing Piety** | +15% Critical Hit Chance for 8s when in Fury. Fury is triggered when 25 enemies have died within 25m. Critical Hits also count up toward triggering Fury. Selecting this Keystone locks all other Keystones. |
| **Martyrdom** | +10% Melee Damage for each missing Wound, up to a maximum of 5 missing Wounds. Selecting this Keystone locks all other Keystones. |

### Keystone Modifiers

**Inexorable Judgement:**
- **Eternal** — Increase the duration of Inexorable Judgement (Momentum) to 10s.
- **Retributor's Stance** — Replenish 0.4% Toughness per second per spent Stack of Momentum during its duration.
- **Inebriate's Poise** — Gain 3 Stacks of Momentum on a successful Dodge.

**Blazing Piety:**
- **Righteous Warrior** — +10% Critical Hit Chance from Blazing Piety (i.e. an extra flat crit chance on top of the keystone's bonus).
- **Infectious Zeal** — Allies in Coherency have +10% Critical Hit Chance while Blazing Piety is active.
- **Invocation of Death** — +100% Ability Cooldown Regeneration for 3s on Melee Critical Hits. *(This talent will lock some other talents.)*
- **Martyr's Purpose** — Up to +50% Ability Cooldown Regeneration based on missing Health (max at 25% current health). *(Locks some other talents.)*
- **Pious Cut-Throat** — +75% Ability Cooldown Regeneration for 2s after a Melee Backstab or Melee Weakspot hit. *(Locks some other talents.)*

**Martyrdom:**
- **I Shall Not Fall** — Martyrdom grants +7.5% Toughness Damage Reduction per stack.
- **On the Brink** — Martyrdom grants +10% Corruption Resistance per stack.
- **Maniac** — Martyrdom grants +6% Attack Speed per stack.
- **Restorative Verses** — Martyrdom grants 5% Toughness Replenishment per stack.

## 6. Build Recommendations (community meta, 2025–2026)

> **Terminology note:** *Fury of the Faithful* and *Chastise the Wicked* are the Zealot's two **combat abilities** (the "F" ability), **not** keystones. The three actual **keystones** are **Inexorable Judgement, Blazing Piety, and Martyrdom** (bottom of the tree). Several third-party guides loosely mislabel abilities as keystones — the in-game tooltips above are authoritative.

### What each keystone is for
- **Blazing Piety** — the most consistently recommended keystone. Crit-engine + cooldown-reduction core; anchors the classic crit → bleed → ability-cooldown loop. Best general/high-difficulty pick.
- **Inexorable Judgement** — aggression/momentum. Buffs melee *and* ranged and can crush Fury's cooldown to ~10–15s, but needs constant movement and a **mobile ranged weapon** (revolver, bolt pistol, shotgun) to keep Momentum flowing. Favored in some Hi-Havoc lists; "expensive" in points.
- **Martyrdom** — glass cannon: ~+8–10% melee damage per missing wound. Rewards low-health play; pair with **Shred** for crit/attack-speed to offset skipping Blazing Piety. High risk, high skill.

### Meta builds

**A) Duelling Sword + Flamer — best all-rounder (beginner-friendly)**
- Keystone: **Blazing Piety** · Ability: **Chastise the Wicked** (Fury of the Faithful equivalent when speccing cooldown)
- Melee: Maccabian Mk IV **Duelling Sword** (*Uncanny Strike + Riposte/Thrust*) — current single-target meta
- Ranged: Artemia Mk III **Purgation Flamer** (*Penetrating Flame + Blaze Away*) — horde clear
- Curios: ~3× **+Toughness (13–17%)**, plus ability-regen / revive speed
- Why: both weapons apply **brittleness**, multiplying each other; melee kills elites, flamer erases hordes. Forgiving and strong at Auric.

**B) "Infodump" survivability crit — Auric/Damnation workhorse**
- Keystone: **Blazing Piety**
- Melee: **Duelling Sword** (*Uncanny Strike + Riposte*) · Ranged: Agripinaa **Braced Autogun** (*Inspiring Barrage*)
- Key defensive nodes (all in the tree above): **Until Death → Holy Revenant** (cheat-death + heal), **Enduring Faith**, **Good Balance**, **Thy Wrath be Swift**, **Bleed for the Emperor**, **Faith's Fortitude**
- Curios: 2× **+20% Max Health**, 1× **+Corruption Resistance**; weapon perks +Flak / +Unyielding
- Philosophy: at Auric/Damnation, **defense > offense** — stack health/toughness and cheat-death rather than min-maxing damage.

**C) Martyrdom melee bruiser — high-skill glass cannon**
- Keystone: **Martyrdom** (+ modifiers *Maniac* attack-speed / *I Shall Not Fall* THDR to survive low HP)
- Melee: Heavy Eviscerator / Chainsword / Duelling Sword with **Shred** · Ability: **Chastise the Wicked** (toughness reset + guaranteed crit)
- Why: damage snowballs as health drops; crit + attack-speed stacking creates a fast melee kill loop. Best for confident dodge/positioning players.

### Weapon shortlist (from sources)
- **Melee:** Duelling Sword (dominant single-target); Chainsword / Heavy Eviscerator / Chainaxe (great with *Shred*); **Thunder Hammer** (elite/monster deletion — 2-shots Daemonhosts).
- **Ranged:** Bolt Pistol / Revolver / Shotgun (mobility → keeps Inexorable Judgement Momentum up); Purgation Flamer (horde); Braced Autogun (toughness sustain).

### Key synergies
- **Chastise loop:** Chastise (50% toughness + guaranteed crit) + toughness-regen nodes keeps you topped up in melee.
- **Crit + Bleed + Cooldown (Blazing Piety):** *Scourge/Blinded by Blood* bleed → more crit vs bleeders → crits feed ability cooldown → more Chastise/Fury uptime.
- **Brittleness stacking:** Duelling Sword (weakspot) + Flamer (impact) both apply brittleness.
- **Momentum + mobility (Inexorable Judgement):** movement buffs melee AND ranged; crit spec slashes Fury cooldown — only with mobile weapons.
- **Shred + attack speed (Martyrdom):** crit stacking compensates for skipping Blazing Piety's crit.
